# Data notice

AirwaveDB is a directory. It does not operate, proxy, or rebroadcast the listed radio services.

Stream endpoints, station names, logos, websites, trademarks, and broadcasts are controlled by their respective operators and rights holders. A listed endpoint may be geo-restricted, rate-limited, moved, changed, or withdrawn without notice.

The database includes public-domain and CC0 directory data from upstream sources. Source attribution is retained in project documentation and source mappings even where attribution is not legally required.

A stream being listed or having passed an availability check does not grant a right to rebroadcast, record, redistribute, or otherwise use the underlying broadcast.
